Privacy Policy for Cozypop Trails

Effective date: August 28, 2026.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Unity cross-platform version of Cozypop Trails published by Omnigame. It does not replace the Privacy Policy used by the currently released native iOS version.

1. Current Unity 1.0.0 Scope

Unity version 1.0.0 is a pure gameplay release. It does not include in-app purchases, third-party advertising, AppsFlyer, Facebook SDK, Firebase, AppLovin MAX, or third-party analytics/attribution SDKs.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The Unity 1.0.0 version does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or payment information.

3. Local Game Data

The app stores gameplay progress, settings, lives, hints, daily challenge state, gallery unlocks, and similar game state locally on your device. This information is used only to run the game experience on your device.

4. Network, Advertising, and Tracking

The Unity 1.0.0 version is intended to run without advertising, attribution, tracking, or analytics SDKs. It does not request App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS and does not use the Android advertising ID for Unity 1.0.0.

5. Sharing

Because the Unity 1.0.0 version does not collect personal data through third-party analytics, ads, attribution, login, or backend services, we do not share such data with third-party advertising, analytics, or attribution partners in this version.

6. Children

The app is a casual puzzle game and is not directed specifically to children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can help.

7. Future Versions

If a future Unity version adds advertising, analytics, attribution, purchases, cloud services, or account features, this Privacy Policy and the store privacy declarations must be updated before release.

8. Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact us at omnigame2026@proton.me.
