What to Include

Unity 1.0.0 Notes

Unity version 1.0.0 is a pure game build. It does not include ads, in-app purchases, account login, cloud save, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Progress and Lives

Progress, lives, hints, daily challenge state, gallery unlocks, and settings are stored locally on your device. Removing the app may remove local progress.
