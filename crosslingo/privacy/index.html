CrossLingo Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 4, 2026 · Version 1.3

CrossLingo ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a translation app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use text, voice, camera, image translation, feedback, attribution, analytics, and CrossLingo Plus subscription features.

1. Summary

CrossLingo does not require you to create an account. The App uses Apple system frameworks for speech recognition, translation, camera capture, photo selection, and text recognition. We do not operate a server that receives your typed text, microphone audio, speech transcripts, translations, photos, or conversation history for translation processing.

CrossLingo Plus subscriptions are provided through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Our servers are used to load subscription catalog information, create purchase orders, verify Apple purchase results, confirm membership status, restore purchases, and diagnose purchase issues.

The App includes an optional feedback feature. If you submit feedback, we collect the feedback text, optional contact information, and optional images that you choose to attach so that we can investigate and reply to your request.

The App uses AppsFlyer, Meta/Facebook SDK, and our own market reporting endpoint for install attribution, marketing measurement, app analytics, and subscription funnel diagnostics. These services must not receive your translation content.

2. Translation Content We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, sell, or share the following information through our own translation services:

  • Voice recordings or live microphone audio
  • Speech transcripts, translations, or conversation history
  • Photos or images captured through the camera for translation
  • Text you type into the App for translation

If you voluntarily attach a screenshot or image to a feedback request, that image is collected as part of customer support rather than translation processing.

8. Local Storage

The App may store basic settings and local app state on your device using iOS app storage. This includes onboarding state, selected languages, subscription catalog cache, daily free translation count, local conversation history if created by the App, and local feedback read-state used to display unread reply indicators. Deleting the App removes its local app container subject to iOS behavior.

9. Purchases and Subscriptions

CrossLingo Plus is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes payment information and manages billing, renewal, cancellation, and refunds. We do not receive your full payment card details.

The App may contact our subscription backend before and after an Apple purchase to create an order, report the Apple transaction, confirm membership, restore active subscriptions, and unlock paid features on this device.

Subscription products, introductory offers, free trials, prices, and renewal terms may vary by app version, region, backend configuration, and App Store settings. The exact terms are shown in the App and in Apple's purchase confirmation sheet before you subscribe.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data can be removed by deleting the App from your device. Subscription and purchase verification records may be retained as needed to provide the subscription, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and support customer service. Feedback records, optional contact information, and feedback images may be retained as needed to process and reply to support requests, prevent abuse, and maintain support history.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Feedback should not include personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date will be revised when material changes are made.
