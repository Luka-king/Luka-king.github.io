CrossTranslate Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026 · Version 1.6

CrossTranslate ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a translation app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use text, voice, camera, image translation, feedback, attribution, analytics, and CrossTranslate Plus subscription features.

1. Summary

CrossTranslate does not require you to create an account. The App uses Apple system frameworks for speech recognition, translation, camera capture, photo selection, and text recognition. We do not operate a server that receives your typed text, microphone audio, speech transcripts, translations, photos, or conversation history for translation processing.

CrossTranslate Plus subscriptions are provided through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Our servers are used to load subscription catalog information, create purchase orders, verify Apple purchase results, confirm membership status, restore purchases, and diagnose purchase issues.

The App includes an optional feedback and support feature. The current in-app Feedback flow lets you choose a support category, write a message, and send an email to our support address through Apple's Mail composer. If the legacy in-app feedback backend is enabled in a later version, it may also collect feedback text, optional contact information, and optional images that you choose to attach.

The App uses AppsFlyer, Meta/Facebook SDK, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and our own market reporting endpoint for install attribution, marketing measurement, app analytics, crash diagnostics, and subscription funnel diagnostics. These services must not receive your translation content.

2. Translation Content We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, sell, or share the following information through our own translation services:

  • Voice recordings or live microphone audio
  • Speech transcripts, translations, or conversation history
  • Photos or images captured through the camera for translation
  • Text you type into the App for translation

If a version of the App allows you to voluntarily attach a screenshot or image to a feedback request, that image is collected as part of customer support rather than translation processing.

8. Local Storage

The App may store basic settings and local app state on your device using iOS app storage. This includes onboarding state, selected languages, subscription catalog cache, daily free translation count, local conversation history if created by the App, local support draft state where applicable, local feedback read-state used for legacy unread reply indicators, and SDK state used for analytics, attribution, crash diagnostics, or subscription reporting. Deleting the App removes its local app container subject to iOS behavior.

9. Purchases and Subscriptions

CrossTranslate Plus is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes payment information and manages billing, renewal, cancellation, and refunds. We do not receive your full payment card details.

The App may contact our subscription backend before and after an Apple purchase to create an order, report the Apple transaction, confirm membership, restore active subscriptions, and unlock paid features on this device.

Subscription products, introductory offers, free trials, prices, retry offers, retention offers, and renewal terms may vary by app version, region, backend configuration, paywall context, and App Store settings. The exact terms are shown in the App and in Apple's purchase confirmation sheet before you subscribe.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data can be removed by deleting the App from your device. Subscription and purchase verification records may be retained as needed to provide the subscription, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and support customer service. Support emails you send may be retained in our email systems as needed to process and reply to support requests. If the legacy in-app feedback backend is enabled, feedback records, optional contact information, and feedback images may be retained as needed to process and reply to support requests, prevent abuse, and maintain support history. Analytics, attribution, crash, and diagnostic records may be retained according to the retention settings of our analytics, attribution, crash reporting, and backend systems.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Feedback should not include personal information from children.
