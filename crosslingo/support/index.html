CrossTranslate Support

Last Updated: October 15, 2026 · Version 1.8

CrossTranslate is a translation app for text, voice, camera, and image translation on iPhone. This page provides support information for CrossTranslate Plus, the in-app Contact us support flow, and the multilingual interface in Version 1.8.

Automatic Input-Language Detection

In versions with automatic detection, you can type, speak, or photograph text in a supported language even when the input selector shows a different language. The input selection is a hint; the target selector determines the output language. For example, Chinese input can be translated into Spanish while the input selector shows English.

Changing the target language retranslates the current input. Photo translation reuses the recognized text when only the target changes. If the input is already in the target language, the original text is kept. For short or unclear input, use a longer phrase, a clearer image, or a quieter recording. Recognition and translation may still make mistakes.

Interface Language

Version 1.8 supports 17 interface languages: English, Spanish, Turkish, Portuguese (Portugal), French, Hindi, German, Italian, Ukrainian, Russian, Filipino, Vietnamese, Korean, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong), and Arabic.

  • Choose the interface language: CrossTranslate follows the first supported language in your iOS language preferences. To change your device preferences, open Settings > General > Language & Region. If iOS offers a Language option under Settings > Apps > CrossTranslate, you can choose a language for this App there. CrossTranslate has no separate in-app interface-language switch.
  • Translation languages: The source and target language selectors inside CrossTranslate control translation, not the interface. Available translation pairs and speech recognition languages may differ from the 17 interface languages and depend on the recognition engine, Apple translation support, and downloaded language resources.
  • Language did not change: Check the App language preference and your preferred language order, then reopen CrossTranslate. If none of your preferred languages is supported, the interface falls back to English.
  • Some content remains in another language: Onboarding animations and sample conversations may contain fixed text. This support page and the linked legal pages are currently provided in English. Text you enter and translation results are not changed by switching the interface language.
  • Prices and dates: Formatting follows the interface language and device region. Changing the interface language does not itself change the subscription price or convert its currency. Check Apple's purchase confirmation sheet before confirming.

Common Help Topics

  • Voice translation: Enable Microphone access in iOS Settings. Versions using Apple speech recognition also need Speech Recognition permission. Automatic voice detection uses a model included with the App, so no separate speech model download is needed. Initial loading can take a moment. If the model cannot load, restart the App and try again. Your microphone audio is recognized on your device and is not uploaded to the speech model provider. Apple translation language packs may still need an internet connection to download.
  • Siri and Dictation message: If iOS reports that Siri and Dictation are disabled, enable Dictation and Siri in system settings and check Screen Time restrictions.
  • Translation availability: Some language pairs may require Apple translation support or language downloads on the device. Try another language pair or follow the in-app download prompt.
  • Camera translation: Make sure Camera access is enabled. Photo Library access is only needed when you choose an image from the system photo picker.
  • CrossTranslate Plus: Subscriptions are purchased through Apple In-App Purchase. Use Restore in the subscription screen if you already subscribed with the same Apple ID.
  • Subscription price or offer: The exact plan, introductory offer, promotional offer, price, billing period, renewal terms, and cancellation terms are shown in the App and in Apple's purchase confirmation sheet before you subscribe.
  • Payment confirmation: Some promotional paywalls may show retry, retention, swipe, or automatic checkout entry points. A subscription is completed only after you confirm it in Apple's purchase sheet.
  • Payment loading: If the payment loading screen remains visible for too long, wait for the Apple sheet or result message. If it does not recover, close and reopen the App, then use Restore if you already subscribed.
  • Translation does not finish: Check the selected language pair and any language-download prompt. If a request fails or times out, try again. If the problem continues, contact support with the affected screen and steps; you do not need to include the private text you were translating.
  • Canceling a subscription: Manage or cancel CrossTranslate Plus from your Apple ID subscription settings. Deleting the App does not cancel an active subscription.
  • Contact us: Open the Profile tab, tap Feedback, choose the category that best matches your request, then send the prepared support email. The draft may include your app version and device context.
  • Support email: If Mail is not configured on your device, use the Copy Draft option in the App or email us directly at the address below. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information, private conversations, payment details, passwords, or confidential content.

Contact Support

The recommended way to contact support is through the in-app Feedback page. It opens a Contact us flow and prepares an email with your selected support category, message, app version, and device context.

You can also contact us by email at aitranslator@yeah.net.

If you cannot access the App, you may open a support issue here:

https://github.com/Luka-king/Luka-king.github.io/issues/new?title=CrossTranslate%20Support%20Request

Please include your device model, full iOS version, CrossTranslate version, interface language, translation source and target languages, and steps to reproduce the issue. For clipped text, right-to-left layout, or unresponsive controls, name the affected screen and include a screenshot only after removing private content. Do not include sensitive personal information, private conversations, or confidential translation content in a public issue.
