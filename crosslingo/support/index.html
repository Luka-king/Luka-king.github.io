CrossTranslate Support

Last Updated: September 22, 2026 · Version 1.6

CrossTranslate is a translation app for text, voice, camera, and image translation on iPhone. This page provides support information for Version 1.6, CrossTranslate Plus, and the in-app Contact us support flow.

Common Help Topics

  • Voice translation: Make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions are enabled for CrossTranslate in the iOS Settings app.
  • Siri and Dictation message: If iOS reports that Siri and Dictation are disabled, enable Dictation and Siri in system settings and check Screen Time restrictions.
  • Translation availability: Some language pairs may require Apple translation support or language downloads on the device. Try another language pair or follow the in-app download prompt.
  • Camera translation: Make sure Camera access is enabled. Photo Library access is only needed when you choose an image from the system photo picker.
  • CrossTranslate Plus: Subscriptions are purchased through Apple In-App Purchase. Use Restore in the subscription screen if you already subscribed with the same Apple ID.
  • Subscription price or offer: The exact plan, introductory offer, promotional offer, price, billing period, renewal terms, and cancellation terms are shown in the App and in Apple's purchase confirmation sheet before you subscribe.
  • Payment confirmation: Some promotional paywalls may show retry, retention, swipe, or automatic checkout entry points. A subscription is completed only after you confirm it in Apple's purchase sheet.
  • Payment loading: If the payment loading screen remains visible for too long, wait for the Apple sheet or result message. If it does not recover, close and reopen the App, then use Restore if you already subscribed.
  • Canceling a subscription: Manage or cancel CrossTranslate Plus from your Apple ID subscription settings. Deleting the App does not cancel an active subscription.
  • Contact us: Open the Profile tab, tap Feedback, choose the category that best matches your request, then send the prepared support email. The draft may include your app version and device context.
  • Support email: If Mail is not configured on your device, use the Copy Draft option in the App or email us directly at the address below. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information, private conversations, payment details, passwords, or confidential content.

Contact Support

The recommended way to contact support is through the in-app Feedback page. It opens a Contact us flow and prepares an email with your selected support category, message, app version, and device context.

You can also contact us by email at aitranslator@yeah.net.

If you cannot access the App, you may open a support issue here:

https://github.com/Luka-king/Luka-king.github.io/issues/new?title=CrossTranslate%20Support%20Request

Please include your device model, iOS version, CrossTranslate version, selected languages, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not include sensitive personal information, private conversations, or confidential translation content in a public issue.
